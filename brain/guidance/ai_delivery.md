# AI Delivery Guidance for High-Stakes Services

Production AI services that reach ministers and senior officials must be reliable, auditable, and continuously monitored once deployed.

Agentic systems should be bounded: a self-directing agent must have hard limits on how far it plans and how many sources it consults, so its behaviour stays predictable.

Every answer produced for a senior decision-maker should be traceable to the evidence that produced it. An audit trail is not optional for tools used in government.

Evaluation rigour is the main barrier to deploying AI in high-stakes settings. A system should be measured on retrieval quality, grounding, and its willingness to abstain when it cannot answer.

Clear communication with non-technical stakeholders is essential to building trust in AI tools across departments.
